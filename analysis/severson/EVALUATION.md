## Notes

- Batch-independent grouped calibration holds out 1 of the 2 training batches and fits on 1. The library warns below 4, so with three batches the grouped-against-random calibration comparison is starved and its coverage figures are reported, not relied on.
- A Severson batch is a test campaign with its own set of charging policies, not a known manufacturing lot.
